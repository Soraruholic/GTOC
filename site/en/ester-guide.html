STEP-BY-STEP / 8 CHECKPOINTS

Build the ethyl acetate line

Follow actual feed, equipment, reaction assumptions and residues in order. Existing single-block test machines and proposed multiblock equipment are different designs.

STEP 01

Prepare feeds and consumables

Feed, devices and operation

Prepare 250 mB acetic acid, 250 mB bioethanol and 1,000 mB water, one catalyst core, one drying core and one coal. /ocplant kit supplies developer consumables; admin tests use /gamemode 0 and at least six free inventory slots. Excess feed stays in F; each new batch takes 1 mol of fresh acid and alcohol.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Use exact fluid names aceticacid, bioethanol and water, without extra fluid tags. A tank's tankContents is container data. This line uses nominal densities at 25 °C; 250 mB is not 0.25 teaching mol. Water is both reaction product and washing medium.

Material destination and checkpoint

Material is still in tanks or inventory. Test commands do not directly credit the OC ledger. Trace survival recipes, machines and power in NEI; do not apply the vitamin A unit convention here.

Devices: plant-f, plant-c, plant-a

Reaction dossiers: No reaction in this step

STEP 02

Place and inspect eight devices

Feed, devices and operation

/ocplant place creates C/F/R/D/W/A/P/T from (X,Y,Z+3), extending along +X, regardless of facing. Fit the catalyst core through C/R, a drying core through A and coal through C. Stay at C's height within 12 blocks; keep every device intact.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Coal supplies 200,000 J of teaching heat; one drying core provides 0.1 mol water-removal capacity. These are model capabilities, not calibrated reaction acceleration. The line controller performs transfers; adjacency is not a fluid pipe network.

Material destination and checkpoint

Expect READY, Catalyst=true, heat=200000 J and dry mol=0.1. Open every right-click panel. Reaction, distillation, washing and drying retain separate material boundaries; side streams remain in T.

Devices: plant-c, plant-f, plant-r, plant-d, plant-w, plant-a, plant-p, plant-t

Reaction dossiers: No reaction in this step

STEP 03

Connect each metered tank to F

Feed, devices and operation

Place one filled Ender IO tank at a time above F: (x+1,y+1,z) relative to C, with the bottom face set to Push. Wait 2–5 seconds, compare the source and /ocplant status, remove only an empty tank, then replace it. An optional real pipe sits between F and a tank two blocks above it.

Chemistry, conditions and time

The OC convention is 1 mB = 1 mL and n = density × volume / molar mass. The common feed limit is 128 mol. Water enters a washing reserve; it is not all charged to R. Bucket pouring and OC product-sample reinjection are unsupported.

Material destination and checkpoint

An initially empty feed ledger should contain about [4.34873400, 4.26023759, 0, 55.34468518] mol for acid/alcohol/ester/water. Transfer waiting is not chemical reaction time. If a tank is empty but F is short, inspect pipe hold-up before adding anything.

Devices: plant-f

Reaction dossiers: No reaction in this step

STEP 04

Observe reversible esterification

Feed, devices and operation

At READY, submit one /ocplant step or the corresponding panel operation and wait for REACTED. Read R's 61-point trace as mol or mole fraction. Charge 1 mol fresh acid and 1 mol fresh ethanol plus the complete returnFeed on recycle batches.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Fischer esterification activates the carbonyl, admits ethanol, transfers protons, loses water and regenerates the acid catalyst. The default teaching setup is 345 K, K=4 and standard reaction enthalpy −4 kJ/mol. dξ/dt = 0.025 n_total (a_acid a_alcohol − a_ester a_water/K), with a_i=γ_i x_i, is integrated for 600 model seconds at 0.25 s steps. These are uncalibrated parameters, not a 600-second real wait.

Material destination and checkpoint

The reference outlet contains 0.489879 mol each acid/alcohol and 0.510121 mol each ester/water. Overlapping curves follow equal amounts. With no volume model, do not label the plot mol/L. The catalyst core is a condition gate, not a real acid concentration.

Devices: plant-c, plant-r

Reaction dossiers: Fischer

STEP 05

Distil while retaining the bottoms

Feed, devices and operation

After REACTED, operate D or use /ocplant step and wait for DISTILLED. Read distillate, bottoms, heating and cooling together.

Chemistry, conditions and time

The model is single-stage Rayleigh batch vapor removal with condensation at 298.15 K, not a refluxed multistage column. At the default 80 kPa, bubble temperature varies with composition. The UNIFAC numerical domain is bounded; acid-vapor association and global VLLE are not solved. There is no calibrated equipment countdown.

Material destination and checkpoint

Reference distillate contains 0.368901 mol ester and bottoms 0.141220 mol. Heating is about 58.523 kJ in this model, without real efficiency. Distillate plus bottoms must equal the complete reactor outlet; distillate is not pure ester.

Devices: plant-d, plant-t

Reaction dossiers: No reaction in this step

STEP 06

Wash and keep both phases

Feed, devices and operation

Run eight washing stages, wash=1/8 through 8/8, with about 4.728568 mol total water from F. Inspect organic and aqueous inventories after each stage. If no split is obtained, retain the full mixture.

Chemistry, conditions and time

At 298.15 K, the bounded original-UNIFAC calculation searches for a lower-Gibbs-energy liquid split. Water removes some acid and alcohol but also dissolves ester. Mixing and settling times are not calibrated; a button performs a calculation, not instantaneous physical settling.

Material destination and checkpoint

After eight washes, about 0.220193 mol ester remains on the product path and 0.148708 mol has entered wash waste. Higher mass purity may reduce recovery. Experimental calibration remains incomplete; keep phase identity and composition together.

Devices: plant-w, plant-t

Reaction dossiers: No reaction in this step

STEP 07

Dry and assess quality

Feed, devices and operation

At WASHED, operate A. The reference batch requires about 0.0613362 mol remaining water-removal capacity. If OFF_SPEC, read the reason; replace/add drying capacity or wash again as appropriate. A rejection retains the wet material.

Chemistry, conditions and time

The model selectively transfers water into the core account. It has no adsorption isotherm, adsorption heat or measured residual-water prediction. The product gate is at least 95 wt% ethyl acetate. More cores add finite capacity, not reaction speed.

Material destination and checkpoint

Reference product amounts are [0.00307143, 0.01243282, 0.22019269, 0] mol, about 96.244 wt% ester. Zero water is an ideal-model result. Product ester yield relative to fresh acid is about 22.02%; purity and yield differ.

Devices: plant-a, plant-p

Reaction dossiers: No reaction in this step

STEP 08

Collect, manage residue and start another batch

Feed, devices and operation

At FINISHED use P or /ocplant bottle with a free inventory slot. This creates one OC quantitative sample of the whole product mixture; it consumes no glass bottle. In T choose 0/50/100% bottoms recycle, prepare the next batch in C, check A's capacity, then start. /ocplant run automates the current batch's 11 calculations; next only prepares it.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Recycle carries acid, alcohol, ester and water, allowing impurities to accumulate. Product, bottoms and wash waste together account for about 0.510121 mol ester in the first reference batch. A recycled batch will have different numbers.

Material destination and checkpoint

The sample cannot be fed back into F and is not a native GT pure-ester item. A full inventory is an optional refusal test. Repeated withdrawal must not duplicate stock, and failures must not lose it.

Devices: plant-p, plant-t, plant-c, plant-a

Reaction dossiers: No reaction in this step
