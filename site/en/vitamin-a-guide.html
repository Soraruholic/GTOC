STEP 01

Prepare the native feeds separately

Feed, devices and operation

/occampaign ionone-kit supplies 25 upstream test items. For C6, use /ocsupply givefeed acetone, /ocsupply givefeed fluid.formaldehyde and /ocsupply givefeed acetylene, one standard cell each. Keep these three apart from the upstream kit; import into the corresponding unit 0 only after construction.

Chemistry, conditions and time

This campaign maps one standard 1,000 mB cell or one full dust to 1 teaching mol. Extra NBT, small/tiny dust, buckets, pipes and generic containers are not accepted by this hand-import path. Cells return empty containers; dust is consumed. Import is a persisted inventory transaction, not a reaction.

Material destination and checkpoint

Native carbon feeds do not prove every auxiliary is survival-supplied. Verify exact item/fluid identity. Missing inputs are rejected; kits are test acquisition, not a survival recipe.

STEP 02

Create six bound sections

Feed, devices and operation

Use an empty flat area for 48 devices. Place in UUID-dependency order: coupling, acetylation, finishing, C6, C14, upstream. Record each UUID and wait for the ledger before the next operation. Recommended rows are four blocks apart, with three clear walkway blocks. Import exactly one held item at a time into the appropriate unit 0 and await confirmation.

Chemistry, conditions and time

The new place-ionone upstream has 16 units; C14, C6, acetylation and finishing have six each, and coupling has eight. Older six/twelve-unit lines retain their model. C14 and C6 are parallel chemical branches; placement order is not reaction order.

Material destination and checkpoint

Bindings require empty associated lines and all blocks loaded/intact. Use /occampaign status. Adjacency does not bind UUIDs or build pipes. The commands create test devices, not a complete survival construction recipe; preserve old worlds.

Devices: upstream-0, darzens-0, supply-0, coupling-0, lines-0, chain-0

STEP 03

U01/U02: make the C5 allylic alcohol

Feed, devices and operation

In upstream unit 0 load the explicit NaNH2 and Pd/CaCO3/Pb test setup; unit 1 performs ethynylation/workup and unit 2 partial hydrogenation/over-reduction. Inspect gas, catalyst and residues in unit 5.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Acetylide attacks acetone to form a C5 alkynol; aqueous workup restores OH. One H2 equivalent reduces the alkyne to an alkene, producing an allylic alcohol, not an enol. Further hydrogenation gives a separately tracked over-reduced alcohol.

Material destination and checkpoint

The reference 0.9 mol alkynol gives 0.82935 mol desired unsaturated alcohol and 0.02565 mol over-reduced alcohol, with all remaining material retained. H2, NH3, NaOH and catalyst stay accounted. No calibrated residence time or atomistic surface trajectory is claimed.

Devices: upstream-0, upstream-1, upstream-2, upstream-5

Reaction dossiers: U01 · U02

STEP 04

A01: prepare methyl chloroacetate in parallel

Feed, devices and operation

Use actual chloroacetic acid, methanol and the specified acid/water account. Operate upstream unit 3; inspect unit 4 without handing off yet.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Fischer esterification retains the chloro substituent. This substrate and its properties differ from ethyl acetate. Temperature, solvent concentration and physical residence time remain uncalibrated.

Material destination and checkpoint

The reference has 0.833 mol methyl chloroacetate in unit 4; all other material remains upstream. Wait for the actual qualified beta-ionone skeleton before C14 handoff.

Devices: upstream-3, upstream-4

Reaction dossiers: A01

STEP 05

U03: form the beta-ketoester

Feed, devices and operation

In unit 6 load the explicit catalyst package, execute Claisen salt formation, then acidification/salt separation. Methyl acetate, sodium methoxide and HCl come from the actual buffer.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Condensation of two ester molecules forms the beta-dicarbonyl system. The acidic product consumes base; restore its neutral form before transfer. Excess-base neutralization also consumes acid.

Material destination and checkpoint

The reference produces 0.9 mol methyl acetoacetate, with methanol and NaCl retained. Distinguish the salt from neutral MAA. A confirmed net operation has no calibrated seconds-scale rate.

Devices: upstream-6, upstream-11

Reaction dossiers: U03

STEP 06

U04: combine C5 and beta-ketoester to C8

Feed, devices and operation

Unit 7 performs the Carroll net operation; unit 8 allocates the C8 pool. Feed the actual C5 allylic alcohol and neutral beta-ketoester. Catalyst and medium remain explicitly test-origin.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Transesterification, [3,3] rearrangement and decarboxylation explain the carbon-skeleton change. CO2 and methanol leave the main skeleton but remain in the overall balance.

Material destination and checkpoint

Reference C8 ketone is 0.66348 mol: 0.630306 mol is transferred and 0.033174 mol remains in unit 7. The 95% component allocation is not validated distillation recovery; real conditions and time remain unknown.

Devices: upstream-7, upstream-8, upstream-11

Reaction dossiers: U04

STEP 07

U05/U06: extend C8 to citral

Feed, devices and operation

Unit 9 performs ethynylation/workup using actual C8 ketone, acetylene, strong base and water. Unit 10 performs a condition-specific net rearrangement with the explicit MoO2(acac)2/Ph3PO/4-tert-butylbenzoic-acid/toluene setup.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Acetylene supplies two carbons; the propargylic-alcohol net rearrangement gives a conjugated aldehyde. It is not a general rule that any acid rearranges any substrate.

Material destination and checkpoint

0.555929892 mol C10 alkynol enters unit 10; the reference citral pool is 0.3335579352 mol with unresolved E/Z. Geranial and neral are comparison identities, not a guessed allocation. No calibrated residence time is supplied.

Devices: upstream-9, upstream-10, upstream-11

Reaction dossiers: U05 · U06

STEP 08

U06 workup and U07 aldol condensation

Feed, devices and operation

Unit 12 first neutralizes the U06 acid with 0.2 mol actual NaOH and performs an ideal citral allocation. Unit 13 then takes 1 mol acetone, 0.01 mol NaOH and 0.01 mol HCl for the U07 operation and neutralization/allocation.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Acetone's alpha carbon attacks citral; aldol addition followed by a candidate E1cb dehydration explains the C13 conjugated ketone. The 95% citral transfer, 80% reaction extent and 95% product transfer are teaching assumptions. 573 J is only the dilute-aqueous strong-acid/base neutralization reference for 0.01 mol.

Material destination and checkpoint

0.31688003844 mol citral enters U07 and 0.2408288292144 mol pseudoionone transfers to unit 14. Catalytic components, salts and organic residues remain in units 10/13. An acidic feed is not pure citral; mol-versus-stage is not mol/s.

Devices: upstream-10, upstream-12, upstream-13

Reaction dossiers: U06-workup · U07 · U07-quench

STEP 09

U08: cyclize, neutralize and qualify beta

Feed, devices and operation

In unit 14 run the alpha/beta net branching, consuming 0.05 mol H2SO4 and then 0.1 mol NaOH for neutralization. Unit 15 performs the beta allocation/quality check. Previously emptied unit 12 becomes the beta qualification pool.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Acid-promoted cyclization and different deprotonation positions explain distinct alpha/beta connectivities. The 80% extent, alpha:beta=30:70 and 95% beta allocation are uncalibrated teaching values. Neither E/Z nor potential stereocentre configurations are assigned.

Material destination and checkpoint

The beta pool is 0.1281209371420608 mol. Residue retains alpha, unreacted pseudoionone, beta remainder, 0.05 mol Na2SO4 and 0.1 mol water. The gate requires beta mole fraction ≥0.98, water/each acid/base ≤0.001 times beta, and a species whitelist. Passing means beta-ionone-unresolved, not an all-trans certificate.

Devices: upstream-14, upstream-15, upstream-12

Reaction dossiers: U08 · U08-alpha · U08-quench

STEP 10

Hand off actual beta and A01 to C14

Feed, devices and operation

With an empty current-model C14 line, use upstream unit 4 to transfer A01 and native auxiliaries. Source and target must confirm together; C14 becomes LOADED. Do not load the old beta test fixture.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Scale the complete transfer by the actual limiting beta/ester inventory. Reject the whole request if an auxiliary is missing. This is a cross-section inventory transaction with no reaction time.

Material destination and checkpoint

Delivered beta is debited upstream; undelivered remainder stays there. Repeated requests return the same result without a second material transfer or rewritten identity/provenance.

Devices: upstream-4, upstream-12, upstream-0, darzens-0

Reaction dossiers: No reaction in this step

STEP 11

A02–A04: build the C14 aldehyde

Feed, devices and operation

C14 unit 1 runs Darzens, unit 2 saponification, unit 3 acidification/neutralization, decarboxylation and conjugation; unit 4 allocates C14. Use the received beta, haloester, methoxide, NaOH, HCl and workup feeds.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Addition and intramolecular SN2 make an epoxide ester; saponification gives its salt. Acidification has electron-flow teaching; later decarboxylation/conjugation remain net endpoints. Conditions and time are not calibrated.

Material destination and checkpoint

Keep qualified C14 separate from salts, methanol, CO2 and unconverted organics. Handoff rounds down to 12 decimal places and retains the remainder. Do not silently assign new stereocentres or C14 E/Z. Wait for qualified C6 before joint delivery.

Devices: darzens-0, darzens-1, darzens-2, darzens-3, darzens-4, darzens-5

Reaction dossiers: A02 · A03 · A04

STEP 12

B01–B04: make C6 in parallel

Feed, devices and operation

C6 unit 0 loads explicit auxiliaries; unit 1 runs aldol addition/dehydration, unit 2 ethynylation/workup, unit 3 allylic rearrangement and unit 4 Z-C6 allocation. Return to C14 unit 4 for the joint real-branch handoff; do not use an old local C14-fixture shortcut.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Build MVK, then perform acetylide 1,2 addition and a candidate allylic rearrangement. Track 1,4 addition, self-condensation and isomer branches separately.

Material destination and checkpoint

Z-C6 and E-C6 are distinct pools. Unit 5 retains water, gas, unreacted material and side products. Z allocation is a model assumption, not an assay. Temperature, concentration, residence time and rates remain uncalibrated.

Devices: supply-0, supply-1, supply-2, supply-3, supply-4, supply-5

Reaction dossiers: B01 · B02 · B03 · B04

STEP 13

C01–C03: activate, couple and work up

Feed, devices and operation

Feed qualified C14 and Z-C6 with effective EtMgBr equivalents. Coupling units: 0 feeds C6, 1 activates both sites, 2 collects ethane, 3 combines feeds, 4 couples, 5 performs acidic workup, then collect further ethane in 2 and separate in 5. Inspect 6/7 for product and residues.

Chemistry, conditions and time

OH and terminal alkyne each consume a reagent equivalent. The terminal carbon attacks the C14 aldehyde; workup restores alcohol groups. Water deactivation and competing ethyl addition also consume equivalents. Formal MgBr balances are not isolated solvated molecules.

Material destination and checkpoint

C20 alkynediol, ethane, formal magnesium salts, unreacted feeds and competing products all remain accounted. The 95% coupling extent is not calibrated. Ethane is evidence of active-hydrogen consumption, not decoration.

Devices: coupling-0, coupling-1, coupling-2, coupling-3, coupling-4, coupling-5, coupling-6, coupling-7

Reaction dossiers: C01 · C02 · C03 · C04

STEP 14

D01/D02: partially hydrogenate and filter

Feed, devices and operation

In finishing unit 0 meter auxiliaries by actual feed and receive C20. Unit 1 partially hydrogenates; unit 2 filters and hands off to acetylation. Hydrogen, catalyst and remaining auxiliaries are still explicit test-origin material.

Chemistry, conditions and time

One H2 equivalent changes the original alkyne to an alkene. Continued reduction yields a distinct over-reduced diol. Catalyst and liquid retained in filter cake remain part of the material balance.

Material destination and checkpoint

The acetylatable diol enters the actual acetylation buffer; over-reduced material, cake and liquid remain behind. Surface-concept teaching does not certify real all-trans selectivity. Physical process conditions remain uncalibrated.

Devices: chain-0, chain-1, chain-2, chain-5

Reaction dossiers: D01 · D02

STEP 15

E01/E02: choose a point on the acetylation curve

Feed, devices and operation

Receive the actual diol and scaled auxiliaries; do not load the old local acetylation fixture. Unit 0 meters to the reactor; unit 1 runs a batch with 300-model-second steps to 3,600 model seconds. After REACTED, discharge, filter and allocate; do not package this intermediate before finishing receives it.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Primary-OH acetylation competes with further secondary-OH acetylation. Read target amount, conversion, selectivity and overall yield separately. The default charge is 303 K with a local 10 L model volume; rate and thermal coefficients are teaching parameters.

Material destination and checkpoint

3,600 model seconds is not one real hour. 60/300-second controls change interaction granularity, not the reaction rules or persistence. Retain monoacetate, diacetate, acetic acid, excess anhydride and cake. Longer reaction is not always better.

Devices: lines-0, lines-1, lines-2, lines-3, lines-4, lines-5

Reaction dossiers: E01 · E02

STEP 16

E03: net rearrangement, crystallization and quality

Feed, devices and operation

Finishing unit 3 receives monoacetate and applies the dehydration/rearrangement net operation. Unit 4 performs crystallization allocation and quality analysis with explicit finishing auxiliaries.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Dehydration, conjugation and geometric allocation are combined net assumptions, not one verified elementary step. Crystals retain entrained material and leave mother liquor. Upstream unresolved E/Z cannot become experimental stereochemical evidence through this calculation.

Material destination and checkpoint

Reference target is about 0.02589196025 mol and one-Z material 0.00007192211 mol. These are model examples. Inspect crystals and mother liquor; distinguish a structural standard, a process assumption and an assay. Real rates remain unknown.

Devices: chain-3, chain-4, chain-5

Reaction dossiers: E03

STEP 17

Package, trace and run a second batch

Feed, devices and operation

With a free inventory slot, package through finishing unit 4 or /occampaign pack <chainUUID>; status returns the package UUID. /occampaign claim <packageUUID> gives a reference certificate; right-click while holding it to inspect origin. No bottle or glass is required. Archive/start the next batch through unit 0 or /occampaign next <chainUUID>, then import 25+3 new feeds.

Chemistry, conditions and time

Packaging transfers the entire impurity-containing stream. Copying/discarding the certificate does not create/destroy stock. Up to 16 histories are retained; a full archive is rejected. Archiving is not physical cleaning, and native GT consumption remains gated.

Material destination and checkpoint

Repeated claim retrieves a reference, not another product quantity. Keep both batches, old mother liquor, gases, catalysts and receipts traceable. Complete the manual UI, full-slot, disconnect/unload and clean-restart checks. Read recovery state before adding more feed.

Devices: chain-4, chain-0, chain-5
