Bilingual chemistry glossary

Precision starts
with the right word.

A translation should preserve the chemical claim, its uncertainty and its units. These are the distinctions used throughout the English edition.

ChineseEnglishMeaning in this project
酯化EsterificationFormation of an ester. The Fischer lesson specifically treats acid-catalyzed carboxylic acid/alcohol esterification.
乙酰化AcetylationIntroduction of an acetyl group, CH₃C(=O)–. The E01 alcohol reaction forms an acetate ester using acetic anhydride.
视黄醇 / 维生素 A 醇RetinolThe alcohol. It is not interchangeable with retinyl acetate in the identity registry.
视黄醇乙酸酯 / 维生素 A 乙酸酯Retinyl acetate / vitamin A acetateThe acetate ester of retinol. The selected target is all-trans-retinyl acetate, with four specified E double bonds in the side chain.
羟醛加成 / 羟醛缩合Aldol addition / aldol condensationAddition forms a beta-hydroxy carbonyl compound. Condensation includes subsequent dehydration. Do not collapse these into one elementary step.
醇盐 / 烯醇盐Alkoxide / enolateAn alkoxide is derived by deprotonating an alcohol. An enolate has charge delocalized across a carbon–carbon–oxygen system derived from an enol/carbonyl compound.
烯醇 / 烯丙醇 / 含烯键的醇Enol / allylic alcohol / unsaturated alcoholIn an enol, OH is attached directly to an alkene carbon. The C5 product 2-methylbut-3-en-2-ol is an allylic alcohol, not an enol.
炔丙醇 / 烯炔醇Propargylic alcohol / enynolPropargylic describes an alcohol-bearing carbon adjacent to an alkyne. Enynol describes a molecule with alkene, alkyne and alcohol functions; it does not mean an enol.
乙炔化EthynylationIntroduction of an ethynyl-derived C₂ unit, here by acetylide addition to a carbonyl. It is unrelated to acetylation.
亲核体 / 亲电体Nucleophile / electrophileAn electron-pair donor / acceptor in the bond-forming step. Curved arrows start at the electron source.
共振贡献式 / 极限式Resonance contributor / limiting representationA representation of a delocalized electronic structure, not a separately isolable intermediate. Formal ionic representations of Grignard species are also bookkeeping approximations.
构型 / 构象Configuration / conformationConformational rotation does not change an E double bond into Z. A chosen conformer does not establish stereoselectivity.
构造异构体 / 立体异构体Constitutional isomer / stereoisomerConstitutional isomers differ in connectivity; stereoisomers differ in spatial arrangement at the same connectivity. Equal formulas do not imply equal identities.
几何异构未定E/Z unspecifiedThe structural record does not assign a configuration or mixture ratio. It does not mean a racemate, a 50:50 E/Z mixture or a freely rotating double bond.
全反式All-transUsed for the specified target identity. Retaining one trans feature upstream does not certify the full product as all-trans.
物质的量 / 浓度Amount of substance / concentrationAmount n is measured in mol. Amount concentration requires a volume basis, such as mol/L. The current reactor's mol curve is not a concentration curve.
摩尔分数 / 质量分数Mole fraction / mass fractionxᵢ = nᵢ / Σnⱼ; wᵢ = mᵢ / Σmⱼ. Both are dimensionless, but their values differ. A wt% specification refers to mass fraction.
转化率 / 收率 / 选择性Conversion / yield / selectivityRespectively: reactant consumption, desired product relative to a feed basis, and product preference on a stated basis. Always specify the stoichiometric basis and denominator.
反应进度Extent of reactionFor a balanced reaction, Δnᵢ = νᵢξ. ξ is in mol; a prescribed fractional progress requires a stated limiting-reactant basis. Do not call every model extent a measured yield.
后处理 / 淬灭Work-up / quenchWork-up prepares the reaction mixture for isolation. A quench consumes/reacts with active reagent. Neither means deleting salts or residual material.
母液 / 釜底 / 馏出物Mother liquor / bottoms / distillateLiquid left after crystal separation / less volatile residual stream from distillation / condensed overhead stream. These are different process outlets.
回收流 / 回流 / 排污Recycle / reflux / purgeRecycle returns material to an earlier process point. Reflux returns condensed overhead liquid to a distillation unit. Purge removes part of a circulating stream. The batch T→C return is called recycle here.
停留时间 / 持料 / 热负荷Residence time / holdup / heat dutyTime associated with material passage / material retained in equipment / required heat-transfer rate. An energy inventory in J is not a heat duty in W.
形式组分 / 有效组分Formal component / effective componentA model identity used to conserve elements, charge or equivalents. It need not be an isolated molecule or a complete description of solution speciation.
测试夹具Test fixtureA defined test input or inventory. A fixture is not evidence that the material has been made from survival-mode resources.
净反应 / 基元步骤Net reaction / elementary stepA net reaction accounts for overall change after intermediates cancel. It does not establish the microscopic path, rate law or number of steps.
过渡态 / 本征反应坐标Transition state (TS) / intrinsic reaction coordinate (IRC)Quantum-chemical pathway concepts requiring appropriate calculations and validation. The website's 3D keyframes are not TS/IRC results.
最高占据 / 最低未占据分子轨道HOMO / LUMOHighest occupied / lowest unoccupied molecular orbital. Interpret them within the stated electronic-structure method; an orbital image alone does not establish a reaction barrier or yield.

Evidence labels are part of the content

Implemented means an operation exists in the current game model. Research, planned and comparison remain distinct. None of these labels alone means an experimentally validated industrial process.

Geometry-screened means that the teaching geometry passed the declared geometric checks. It does not mean the structure is an optimized solution intermediate. Literature conditions and patent yields retain their original scope.

About this English edition

The English learning pages are maintained as a separate static edition. The Wiki and classrooms use the same source catalog and molecular assets as the Chinese pages. IDs, SMILES, formulas, numerical inventories, mapped atoms, bond orders, coordinates and SVG/SDF structures are preserved.

Translations match exact source text. A newly added or changed Chinese passage without an approved English entry displays English review pending, with a link to the original. It does not silently inherit an older translation based only on its record ID.

The glossary and translations have been checked for consistency within this implementation. They have not received an independent chemistry review. Historical engineering records, full campaign acceptance manuals and some specialized process simulators remain in Chinese and are labeled accordingly.
