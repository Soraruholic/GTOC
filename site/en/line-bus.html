05 / PLAYABLE PRODUCTION

From dedicated stores to two finished batches

/ocfactory dedicated ester/ocfactory dedicated vitamin_a/ocfactory recipes/ocfactory complete-status
  1. Start GTNH-OC 0.4.8 Dedicated (isolated test) in Prism. Both client and server must use 0.4.8-dedicated-alpha1. Use the configured test-server entry; with the local SSH tunnel active its endpoint is 127.0.0.1:25566.
  2. In clear loaded space run /ocfactory dedicated ester or /ocfactory dedicated vitamin_a. The anchor is player position + (−45,+3,−40). Reserve about 104×74 or 104×198 blocks respectively. Inspect the space in creative flight first and keep clear of older factories.
  3. Record the UUID, anchor and step using /ocfactory complete-status. Repeating dedicated returns the same plant. Empty stores wait at a feed gate. Inspect store identity and phase; an acid packet in the ethanol inlet must stay uncredited, never becoming ethanol stock.
  4. Use NEI to craft ceil(2 × per-batch demand) packets of each material from the table below. Feed each matching store chest manually or through item logistics. Machines meter automatically from these stores. Catalysts, drying media and heater packets each have their own store.
  5. The table already includes one treatment service packet per batch: two for this check. Supply its dedicated store; do not double-count it. Missing treatment supply retains residues until replenished. A 1000 mB packet conversion is a recipe-level game convention, not a molar volume.
  6. Inspect Overview, Materials, Trends and Diagnostics on a process unit. Pause/resume controls the line. With stock and loaded chunks the scheduler admits at most one operation per second: 13 ester or 70 vitamin-A steps, plus hopper, persistence and construction time. Trends show observed inventory and the bar shows sequence progress.
  7. Inspect stream, source and destination at a pipe. Blue incoming and green outgoing faces follow the same line; neighbouring lines with different identities stay separate. Use Pause during ordinary inspection. Destructive structure checks belong in a backed-up test world; restoring a pipe requires its full line identity data.
  8. After batch one, inspect whole-stream receiver composition, qualified reclaim and disposal. Pipes do not sort mixtures. Only approved single-species streams return to the matching store under the 95% game rule. With supply, continue into batch two; verify batch=2 and preserved history.
  9. Claim retinol uses with /ocfactory products <UUID>. Right-click the held reference for 12 minutes of game night vision; an existing effect prevents another debit. Copies share one balance. Whole ester product enters the OC dispatch depot; inspect cumulative composition in the service UI.
  10. Record stock, batch, product uses and residues before disconnecting, unloading and restarting. Unloaded chunks stop progress; reloading resumes from the same inventory. Repeated claims or build commands must not duplicate stock.
  11. Use /ocfactory hold-products <UUID> to pause ester dispatch; a full store must stop progress. Resume with /ocfactory ship-products <UUID>. Retinol credit holds up to 8192 uses. Save observations from both batches as your client acceptance record.

06 / CONTINUOUS OPERATION

A destination for every stream. Then another batch.

01 · Separate treatment

Known acids and base in the same stream neutralise by equivalents. Separate vessels are never pooled. Reactive residues, gases and metal-bearing cake retain their identities.

02 · 95% qualified reclaim

Only approved single-species streams qualify. 95% returns to the same species store; 5% is purged. Mixtures are never automatically certified as pure solvent.

03 · Paid dispatch

Consume one treatment service packet per batch. Remaining waste goes to the contracted disposal ledger and whole ester product to the OC dispatch depot. Retinol retains usable night-vision credit.
