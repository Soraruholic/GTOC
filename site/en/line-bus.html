Aqueous effluent

Sources: Salts, wash water, acid/base residues

Collect separately; assess salts, solvents and metals. Acceptable pH alone does not permit discharge.

COLLECT_ONLY

Organic mother liquor

Sources: Alcohol, ester, solvent and isomer mixtures

Retain compatibility and lot identity; recovery needs a separation model and quality certificate. Keep the bottoms.

RECOVERY_REVIEW

Reactive residues

Sources: Grignard, strong base, active-metal residues

Deactivate or quench locally under a specific model before central transfer. Without an adapter, hold locally.

LOCAL_REQUIRED

Off-gas

Sources: Ammonia, acid gas, unknown gas

Control and capture at source. Do not combine incompatible gases. Account for absorbent, outlet gas and bleed.

LOCAL_REQUIRED

Catalyst / filter cake

Sources: Metal, support, entrained mother liquor

Segregate in closed receivers; retain reactive material locally. Preserve loading and entrained-liquid accounts.

LOCAL_REQUIRED

Unknown / off-spec

Sources: Missing composition, identity or provenance

Dedicated quarantine by phase. Quarantine is neither neutralization nor destruction. Full receivers stop transfer.

QUARANTINE

Three recovery gates

Collection is not recovery. ① Confirm composition and compatibility → ② Apply a validated separation or treatment with energy, reagent, entrainment and residue accounts → ③ Requalify recovered stock for its intended use. Implemented code provides finite collection, quarantine, backpressure and recovery hold; unknown recovery efficiency, treatment chemistry and heat remain unknown.

RECOVERY STARTS WITH ACCOUNTING

Reuse candidate and purge: impurities remain in both.

Central whole-mixture splitting is implemented. Apply the same operator-selected fraction to every component; this is not distillation, extraction or purification. An 80% split does not turn 90% purity into 100%. Reuse remains quarantined and purge remains in a finite waste receiver. Occupied or undersized outputs reject the entire operation without a debit. Reactive, unknown and product-hold lanes are ineligible.

The one-command build also places recovery-reuse and recovery-purge storage ports. Splitting shares the single writer, durable receipts and authenticated player identity. The inspect report retains input/output composition, source revision, provenance, fraction and request UUID. Heat remains unknown and is not converted to EU; no route sends reuse stock to SOURCE.

/ocfactory recover <factory UUID> organic 80 <request UUID>
/ocfactory recover <factory UUID> residue 80 <request UUID>

Choose organic for ester or residue for vitamin A. Use a new UUID for a new operation and the same UUID for replay. An empty receiver returns RECOVERY_EMPTY. Chemistry output adapters are still pending; do not fabricate waste in production stores for a demonstration. The isolated selftest covers impurity-preserving splits, capacity refusal, local safety, replay and persistence recovery.

MATCH THE COMMAND TO THE WORLD

How is the test rack actually laid out?

This view follows the same LineBus.position rule: one-block storage ports, 2-block X spacing and 3-block Z spacing. Numbers follow deterministic node order. It is not a preview of a complete multiblock chemistry plant. Drag to rotate, select a port, or use the keyboard controls.

SOURCE receives external feed; FEED_BUFFER holds metered stock; LOCAL_BUFFER is a process-adapter boundary; CENTRAL_HOLD is segregated collection. Transfers use ledger transactions, not imaginary native pipes.

EXECUTABLE INTEGRATION RACK

One command builds the bus test rack.

Use an administrator client matching the line-bus build. Execute either command in a clear area. It places real storage ports and enables scheduling; it grants no feed and does not place an entire chemistry plant.

/ocfactory integration ester
/ocfactory integration vitamin_a
  1. Keep X+2…X+16 and Z+3…Z+18 clear at your standing height. Existing blocks are never overwritten.
  2. Wait for the ledger commit and port placement. Repeating the build for the same owner/product reuses its factory and preserves stock.
  3. First check MISSING_FEED. Right-click a port for node, feed and collection status. Identity filters remain separate.
  4. Connect already supplied GT / EnderIO tanks to SOURCE. Observe automatic transfer into FEED_BUFFER. CHARGED means the first feed buffer is ready, not that chemistry has finished.
  5. Reports live under oc-factory-tests/ in the world directory. Isolated model tests declare testFixture=true and productionInventory=false; no fixture enters player or production inventory.
  6. Record the factory UUID; check the same amounts after reconnect, unload/reload and restart. Removing a committed port pauses the rack; it does not duplicate blocks.
/ocfactory diagnose <factory UUID>
/ocfactory inspect <factory UUID>

inspect exports real nodes, capacities, routes and provenance. Self-test reports separately list persistence fault recovery. Human checks still cover UI readability, tank orientation, external pipes and multiplayer use.

Delivered scope: line-level storage / collection bus and isolated integration checks. This command does not establish a fully automatic chemistry plant, effluent purification, solvent-recovery equipment or standard GT product dispatch.
