A destination for every stream. Then another batch.

01 · Separate treatment

Known acids and base in the same stream neutralise by equivalents. Separate vessels are never pooled. Reactive residues, gases and metal-bearing cake retain their identities.

02 · 95% qualified reclaim

Only approved single-species streams qualify. 95% returns to the same species store; 5% is purged. Mixtures are never automatically certified as pure solvent.

03 · Paid dispatch

Consume one treatment service packet per batch. Remaining waste goes to the contracted disposal ledger and whole ester product to the OC dispatch depot. Retinol retains usable night-vision credit.

Run and recover step by step

  1. Build with /ocfactory production ester or vitamin_a. New plants include three service modules; older plants and stocks remain intact. Repeating the command returns the same new plant.
  2. Feed process packets and one treatment service packet per batch into the central double chest. Right-click service:0/1/2 for state and cumulative destinations.
  3. Missing treatment supply retains every residue. Replenishment resumes automatically. Unloaded chunks or a broken rack block the next commit.
  4. Use /ocfactory hold-products to retain ester product locally until the store fills; /ocfactory ship-products resumes dispatch. The depot is a game logistics endpoint in the ledger, with visible composition and no creation of native pure GT material.
  5. Record treatment batches, stores, reclaim, disposal and product credit before restart. Matching state can continue. Missing or damaged history fences the system to prevent duplicate credits.
Teaching basis and simplifications

Each stream can apply HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O, H₂SO₄ + 2NaOH → Na₂SO₄ + 2H₂O, or CH₃COOH + NaOH → CH₃COONa + H₂O. These do not destroy remaining organics. Separate wastes stay separate; contaminated solvent and metal catalysts do not automatically return to feed. Paid contracted disposal represents complex final treatment without pretending to simulate every incineration, biological, off-gas and sludge operation.

Game recipe power and thermodynamic evidence are recorded separately. Unmeasured enthalpies remain unknown; fixed gameplay costs can still run.
