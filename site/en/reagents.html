Feedstocks and OC recipes

Every purchased feed and auxiliary has an OC-added recipe in a GT machine. The main route makes its intermediates, including beta-ionone.

Who adds the recipe, and where does its feed come from?

All 37 recipes on this page are added by OC to the GT Large Chemical Reactor: 20 packaging conversions, 13 aggregate syntheses and 4 gameplay consumables. Native inputs do not make a recipe native. Input registration and recipe matching were checked in this pack; use its NEI for native upstream production.

All current recipes are retained. Future dedicated lines should initially coexist, with versioned costs and unlocks; existing packet and stock identities must remain readable.

Craft packets, then feed the central chest

Search NEI for a packet or species ID below. Feed liquids and gases through LCR input hatches and dust/packets through item buses. Route packet output via item transport or hoppers to the OC double chest. Collect fluid coproducts in separate output hatches and tanks. Circuits are reusable. Multi-step synthesis recipes are aggregate game processes, not single elementary reactions.

How to read these process rules

One packet equals one game process unit. The 1000 mB conversion is a gameplay exchange, not a density or molar-volume claim. Reagents and listed coproducts participate in recipe accounting. Kinetics, pressure-drop and thermophysical evidence belongs on the model-reference page; mechanism teaching is not inferred automatically from NEI recipes.

Expand aggregate recipes into supply lines

These are engineering candidates for defining equipment, streams and research scope, not completed plants or laboratory procedures. Prioritize sodium methoxide, then isopropanol/aluminium alkoxide, then ligand/molybdenum catalyst supply; rank the rest by demand and recovery value.

Bases and alkoxides

Separate sodium/ammonia and sodium/methanol reactors → segregated hydrogen collection → solvent/solid separation → sealed dry feed. Do not share aqueous storage.

Split sodium methoxide first for C6 and ligand supply; give sodium amide its own ammonia recovery and dry boundary.

sodium-amide · sodium-methoxide

Isopropanol and aluminium alkoxide

Acetone hydrogenation → gas/liquid split and hydrogen recycle → alcohol/ketone separation → drying → aluminium alkoxide synthesis → hydrogen collection and solvent recovery. Model activation and catalysts separately.

Connect to MPV supply with explicit water limits, alcohol/ketone recycle and purge.

isopropanol · aluminium-isopropoxide

Cyclic carbonate supply

Catalytic ethylene oxide/CO₂ cycloaddition → gas recovery → catalyst separation → ethylene carbonate purification; collect water-related byproducts such as ethylene glycol separately.

Provide an independent U03 supply unit before comparing consumption and catalyst life.

ethylene-carbonate

Bromoethane and Grignard reagent

HBr supply → ethanol substitution → acid/water separation → bromoethane purification and drying → magnesium insertion in dry ether → sealed transfer; locally quench reactive magnesium residues before brine treatment.

Ether is a solvent. Equal ether input/output in the current recipe is a carrier rule, not evidence of 100% plant recovery.

ethyl-bromide · ethylmagnesium-bromide

Beta-diketone ligand and molybdenum catalyst

Ketone/ester condensation → acidification → brine separation → acetylacetone purification → coordination with a verified Mo(VI) precursor → solid/liquid separation and mother-liquor recovery.

Metal molybdenum + oxygen + ligand is an aggregate game recipe. A dedicated line needs explicit oxidation-state conversion and a precursor.

acetylacetone · molybdenyl-acetylacetonate

Nitrogen heterocycles

Pyridine: catalytic aldehyde/ammonia condensation, gas recycle and fractionation of heterocycle mixtures. Quinoline: a candidate Skraup route with explicit acid catalysis, oxidant, workup neutralization and nitrogen-containing wastewater.

A net equation cannot establish a single isomer or pure product. Start with mixture identity and separation checks.

pyridine · quinoline

Substituted aromatic acid

Isobutyraldehyde → isobutanol → isobutene; toluene alkylation → ortho/para separation → side-chain oxidation → acid crystallization and mother-liquor collection.

The current structure is explicitly 4-tert-butylbenzoic acid. A dedicated line must separate and qualify the para isomer rather than label a crude alkylation mixture as pure para material.

tert-butylbenzoic-acid

Organophosphorus reagents and recovery

Chlorobenzene → phenylmagnesium reagent → PCl₃ substitution and salt separation → triphenylphosphine → oxidation to its oxide; recovery must distinguish phosphine from phosphine oxide.

Segregate magnesium salts, ether solvent and phosphorus residues. Phosphine oxide cannot substitute for equally active phosphine.

triphenylphosphine-oxide

Acceptance for each supply line

  1. Verify native feed identities, net chemistry and catalyst roles; aggregate accounting must not stand in for plant operation.
  2. Separate reaction, separation, recycle, local treatment and central disposal; bind each stream to a finite receiver.
  3. Define quality gates, game power costs and stop conditions; test missing feed, full receivers, two batches and restart before replacing the aggregate recipe.
