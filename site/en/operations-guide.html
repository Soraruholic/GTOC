Four steps from an order to a clean stop

01

Set the order

Right-click a process unit → Supplies → 1 / 2 / 4 batches. The current unfinished batch counts; an already settled batch does not. Selecting again replaces the target.

02

Replenish the shortfall

The supplies tab shows stock, remaining demand, deficit, rounded-up packet count and chest coordinates. Immediate shortages come first. Consumed feed is not bought again; future reclaim is not prepaid stock.

03

Read the cause, then resume

Overview distinguishes isolation, pause, shortages, structure and full receivers with an action hint. Restore the original line and clearances before reopening. Pause retains the order; resume adds neither feed nor extra batches.

04

Finish workup before stopping

After the target batch completes its service, reclaim and dispatch settlement, automatic production stops. Missing service supply or receiving space waits; it does not complete the order early. Choose another order or explicitly select Continuous to continue.

A repeatable client check

  1. Confirm alpha4 on both sides and the five tabs: Overview, Materials, Trends, Supplies and Diagnostics.
  2. Choose two batches and record the target. Verify service packets are included; crediting one packet reduces the deficit while retaining fractional stock.
  3. Pause, isolate and reconnect: the target persists and Resume cannot bypass a closed valve.
  4. Supply and reopen. Verify the plant stops after both workups; wait five seconds without further debit. Resume should require a new order.
  5. Start another order. After restart compare batch, target and stock. Test missing service, full receivers and duplicate requests in a copied test world.
  6. Craft and place all seven access variants; inspect recovered metadata, climbing and clearances without breaking the live plant pipes.
