Understand the logistics rules

A collection of dedicated stores

Acid, alcohol, water, hydrogen and each catalyst/consumable have separate stores, chests, hoppers and filtered inlets. Wrong materials are rejected. Empty stores retain their fixed identity.

One line, one destination

Every line binds a fixed stream, source and destination. Supply, process, collection and qualified reclaim use separate paths. Blue collars mark the incoming face; green marks outgoing. Right-click a segment for direction. Adjacent lines do not connect automatically; rises and horizontal runs follow the frozen path.

Transfer is a guarded stock handoff

The server validates structure and lines before debiting each operation. Broken/reversed lines, missing stock or unloaded chunks stop progress. This phase does not model flow rate or pressure loss; solids and consumables use dedicated enclosed supply channels.

Separate stores, receivers and lines

Central single-material store

Every raw store has an immutable identity, phase, capacity and inlet filter. The acetic-acid store accepts only acetic acid and the ethanol store only ethanol; an empty store never changes identity. The server ledger remains authoritative; the block is a visible port.

Fixed-stream receiver

A reactor outlet may be a mixture, but it enters an isolated receiver as one named process stream. The receiver does not split acetic acid, ethanol, ethyl acetate and water into four pipes; separation, distillation and waste treatment belong to downstream equipment.

Minimum state of a line block

Each pipe block stores a line UUID, fixed stream identity, duty, phase, incoming face and outgoing face. Adjacency does not imply connection; the server validates every cell before debit and rejects gaps, reversed segments or wrong labels.

Recognise equipment by duty

Reactors and columns

Jacketed reactors have vertical bodies, support frames and top drives; columns use narrow banded bodies. Hydrogenation, filtration, drying, crystallisation and buffering have distinct duty classes and port textures.

Exchangers and stores

Exchangers and separators use horizontal vessels. Source stores and fixed-stream receivers are 5×5×5 multiblocks: vertical liquid tanks, horizontal gas vessels and cone-bottom solids bins. Process modules use a 7×5×7 test envelope; geometry supports widths 3/5/7 and heights 4–8.

Industrial silhouettes

Original meshes and pixel textures use frames, nozzles, flanges, drives and supports to explain equipment. Size does not automatically imply real volume, pressure rating or chemical yield.

Right-click to inspect and control

Overview / Materials / Trends / Diagnostics

Overview shows sequence progress, the next operation and blocking reason. Materials distinguishes local custody from source stocks. Pause/resume controls the whole line. Storage has a fill gauge and paginated diagnostics.

Plot observed data

Trends sample local material amounts every 0.5 seconds for up to 60 seconds and reset between batches. Amounts are not concentrations without volume data. Sequence progress is not chemical conversion. Controls validate the real player, distance, window session and sequence on the server.

Start with empty dedicated stores

1 · Construct the dedicated plant

Run /ocfactory dedicated ester or /ocfactory dedicated vitamin_a as an operator. Full structure envelope: ester 104×74 blocks, 9 high; vitamin A 104×198, 12 high, including stores and receivers. Anchor: player position (−45,+3,−40). Every cell must be clear and loaded. Repeating the command returns the existing dedicated plant.

2 · Supply each store

Find OC reagent packet recipes in NEI and craft them in the GT Large Chemical Reactor. Supply the chest in front of each matching store. Catalyst, drying, heater and treatment consumables have dedicated stores too. Packets credit process units; this build does not convert every ingredient into native Forge fluid.

3 · Inspect and run

Inspect stock at each store and identity/direction at each pipe. Open a process module and resume the line. The scheduler advances at most one operation per second and waits for missing supplies. Use Materials and Trends to observe the process.

4 · Products and residues

Existing quality gates and residue accounts remain. Claim retinol credentials with /ocfactory products FACTORY_UUID. At collection, fixed-stream receivers take custody of complete composition accounts. Each receiver has an isolated treatment contract. Mixtures are not automatically sorted. Qualified single-species reclaim follows that receiver’s dedicated return path.

One dedicated pipe, one fixed process stream

Fixed identity; composition follows the process

Esterification effluent contains acid, alcohol, ester and water. It travels as one named process stream. Its pipe does not split or purify components; downstream separation belongs to the equipment model.

Segregated reception and reclaim

Each rear receiver binds one terminal-stream ID, with vessels appropriate for gas, liquid or solids. Equipment UI relinquishes custody after collection. Treatment checks each receiver separately: qualified single-species material returns at a bounded recovery fraction; other material stays segregated for paid dispatch. Shared service modules provide consumable, quality and dispatch controls, not a common mixed waste vessel.

Transport and chemistry have separate responsibilities

This release uses atomic stock handoffs at operation boundaries. Physical paths must be intact; fluid is not integrated through each pipe every tick. Kinetics and equilibrium retain their model-specific basis. Local valves, dosing branches and cleaning operations precede flow-rate and pressure-loss gameplay.
